Instructions — how to rate (click to collapse)

Listen to each recording (a sustained /a/ vowel) and rate the voice on the five GRBAS dimensions. Severity scale for every dimension: 0 normal · 1 slight · 2 moderate · 3 severe.

  • G — Grade: overall severity of dysphonia (usually reflects the most affected dimension; need not equal their sum).
  • R — Roughness: irregularity of vocal fold vibration.
  • B — Breathiness: audible air escape.
  • A — Asthenia: vocal weakness / hypofunction.
  • S — Strain: effort / hyperfunction.
  • Recordings are 8 kHz — fine for G/R/B/A/S; treat very high-frequency detail with caution. Ignore recording loudness.
  • If a recording is noisy/clipped/wrong-task, set usable = no, add a comment, and you may leave G/R/B/A/S blank.
  • The diagnosis column is the database's medical label, not a GRBAS hint — rate by ear, blind to it where you can.
  • Your work auto-saves in this browser. When finished, click Export results (CSV) and send the downloaded file back.
